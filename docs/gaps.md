# Gaps: what NoteCraft lacks, ranked

> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-14 · **Last updated:** 2026-10-14 · **Change:** major (first version)

The ranked work list. The exhaustive per-control list is the generated
[`parity-checklist.md`](parity-checklist.md) (missing P0/P1 rows); this page ranks the big pieces.

| # | Gap | Why it matters | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everything in M1: shell, hierarchy, canvas editing, tags, search, persistence | The 80% daily workflow | M1 |
| 2 | Insert and Draw tabs: tables, pictures, files, ink | Second most used surface | M2 |
| 3 | `.one`/`.onetoc2`/`.onepkg` import and export | Switching needs the user's existing notebooks | M3 |
| 4 | History, Review, View depth; passwords; options; accessibility | Power users and teams | M4 |
